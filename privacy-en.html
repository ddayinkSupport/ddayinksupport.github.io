Privacy policy

Scope and operator

This policy covers the current development and test versions of DdayInk and its support website. Operator: Wooseong Cho (individual developer). Privacy contact: ddayink.support@gmail.com. Effective date: November 16, 2026.

On-device information

Event titles, notes, dates, recurrence rules, artwork, reminders and display preferences are stored on your device. There is no app account or developer-hosted event or photo upload service. The app stores a reduced copy of a photo you choose through the system picker without altering the original. In-app iCloud sync is not available. Device backups may include app data according to your operating system settings.

Widgets and reminders

The app and its widgets share the event details and artwork needed for display locally on the device. Private notes are excluded from the widget snapshot. With permission, reminders are scheduled through the device notification system. Event details may appear in notifications and widgets. Manage visibility in device notification and Lock Screen settings.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. The app checks transaction and entitlement information from StoreKit to apply ad removal. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment passwords directly. Restoring purchases restores ad-removal access, not events or photos.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform. The SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, crash information, performance and diagnostics. IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location. Processing supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Event titles, notes and photos are not supplied as ad-targeting data. The app requests nonpersonalized ads; this does not mean no data processing. The current app does not request ATT permission. Consent status and permission to request ads are checked before ad requests. Where applicable, advertising privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Once a purchased ad-removal entitlement is verified, the app stops making new ad requests. This does not delete data previously processed by SDK providers.

Current service status and providers

Development builds currently use official Google test ads; production advertising is not yet offered. Test ads can still involve SDK data processing. Google advertising, Gmail and GitHub hosting may process information on servers outside your country, including the United States. See the Google and GitHub privacy policies below for their processing, retention and deletion practices. This policy will be updated to reflect actual processing before production advertising or other service changes begin.

Support and website

In-app bug reporting is hidden for the first release and does not provide report submission. This support website has no submission form, analytics scripts, ads or third-party fonts. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement. The developer does not install separate visitor analytics scripts. Support uses Gmail at ddayink.support@gmail.com. Sender email addresses and inquiry content are used to respond and troubleshoot. Inquiry emails are deleted within 90 days after the inquiry is closed, except where legal retention obligations require keeping them for the necessary scope and period. Relevant messages in the developer-managed inbox and sent folder are deleted and removed from Trash. Provider backup retention and deletion follow the provider policies.

Retention and deletion

Deleting an event removes it from the active list and updates widgets and reminders. Older local data-migration backups and device backups may retain earlier copies. Event deletion does not immediately erase every backup. App removal and device backups follow operating system behavior and settings. The developer cannot view or delete your device-only events from a server.

Choices and requests

Manage photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Advertising privacy options are available in the app where required. The privacy contact, ddayink.support@gmail.com, will explain how to make access, correction, deletion or restriction requests under applicable law. Device-only information must be managed on your device. Where possible, send your request from the email address used for your inquiry. We use only the information needed to identify the request and respond under applicable law.

Changes

This policy will be updated if processing changes, including future sync or support submission features. Updated policies and effective dates are published on this page; material changes are also announced in the app or support page where appropriate.
