How can we help?

What is DdayInk?

A place for anniversaries, birthdays, remembrance dates and recurring events, decorated with photos and lettering and displayed in widgets.

How many events are free?

The first event needs no ad. From the second event, complete one rewarded video ad to add one event. Editing and deleting saved events need no ad. List ads may also appear. There are no in-app purchases.

An ad will not load.

Check your internet connection and network filtering settings, then try again later. Ad availability and provider conditions can also prevent loading. Without completing the ad, a new event is not saved, but your open editor keeps its draft. Check your content before closing the app.

Which lunar calendar is used?

The Korean lunar calendar. Changing the language does not switch to Chinese or historical Japanese calendars. Check the regular/leap-month and short-month options for your event.

How do I decorate photos?

Choose a photo in the event editor, then adjust the thumbnail shape and position and the text size, color and placement. Choosing an emoji with a photo present keeps the photo as part of the decoration.

Reminders or widgets are missing.

Check notification permission and Focus settings in iOS. Add widgets through Home Screen or Lock Screen editing. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so updates may not appear immediately.

Will events sync to a new device?

In-app iCloud sync is not available yet. Data is stored on your device; device backup and restoration follow Apple settings. Do not delete the app as your first troubleshooting step.

How can I contact support?

Contact ddayink.support@gmail.com. DdayInk is operated by Wooseong Cho, an individual developer. In-app report submission is not currently available. Useful details include app version, iOS version and reproduction steps. Passwords, private events and personal photos are not needed.
